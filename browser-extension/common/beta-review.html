Normal browsing beta review

Development-only helper for the v1.5 manual browsing gate. It never stores full URLs, paths, titles or raw hostnames.

Local evidence

Coverage readiness is not Release readiness. Exported evidence remains unbound to the final ZIP SHA-256 and cannot close the release gate by itself.

Privacy contract

Only a 16-hex SHA-256 prefix of the entered hostname plus test flags is stored locally. No telemetry, no network upload, no page URL, path, query, fragment, title or note text is retained.
